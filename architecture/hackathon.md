# Hackathon Brief

## SDG 4: Quality Education

**Real-World Context:**
In many classrooms, especially those with limited resources, one lesson plan is delivered to a room full of students at very different skill levels. Teachers rarely have visibility into which specific concepts each student is struggling with until test results come back — often too late to intervene meaningfully. This is worsened in low-resource schools, where teachers manage large classes with little time or tooling to track individual progress. As a result, struggling students fall further behind while faster students plateau, and neither gets material matched to their actual level.

**Problem Statement:**
Teachers lack real-time visibility into where students are struggling, and practice materials don't adapt to individual skill levels.

**Who is Affected?**
- Students in under-resourced classrooms
- Teachers with large class sizes and limited tracking tools
- School administrators planning intervention/resource allocation

**Challenge**
How might we give every student personalized practice while showing teachers exactly where the class is struggling?

**Suggested Areas**
- Personalised learning activities
- Identifying students' learning gaps
- Teacher-friendly progress tracking
- Learning support for limited connectivity

**Expected Outcomes**
A functional prototype showing adaptive practice per student, plus a simple dashboard giving teachers an at-a-glance view of class-wide learning gaps.
